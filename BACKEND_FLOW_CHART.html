3 Mathematical Formulations & Computational Logic
Algorithmic Deep Dive

📐 1. Perceptual Color Distance (CIE76 ΔE)

Scikit-Learn KDTree

Measures Euclidean distance between candidate product dominant color and target market style palettes in the non-linear CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ color space:

ΔE = √[ (L*&sub1; - L*&sub2;)² + (a*&sub1; - a*&sub2;)² + (b*&sub1; - b*&sub2;)² ]
  • L* (Lightness): 0 (black) to 100 (diffuse white)
  • a* (Green-Red Axis): negative = green, positive = red
  • b* (Blue-Yellow Axis): negative = blue, positive = yellow
  • Target Threshold: ΔE ≤ 13.5 denotes acceptable fashion harmony

🎯 2. Compatibility Score Normalization

Normalized Score (0..1)

Converts unbounded Euclidean color distance into a bounded $[0, 1]$ suitability index, weighting dominant tone higher than average palette distance:

Score = max(0.0, 1.0 - (ΔE / 30.0))
Composite = 0.60 × Score_dominant + 0.40 × Score_mean
  • Score ≥ 0.70: High color alignment (Prime candidate)
  • Score ≥ 0.60: Standard market fit (Approved)
  • Score < 0.60: Outlier / Non-priority tone

⚖️ 3. Multi-Tier Marketing Rules

DSS Rule Engine

Assigns marketing priority tiers based on simultaneous evaluation of perceptual color match, sales readiness, and demographic fit:

IF (ΔE ≤ 8.5 & Score ≥ 0.70 & ready_to_sale) → Tier 1 (Prime)
ELSE IF (ΔE ≤ 13.5 & Score ≥ 0.60) → Tier 2 (Standard)
ELSE → Tier 3 (Non-Priority Outlier)
  • Tier 1 Action: Immediate Hero Product Ads & Paid Acquisition
  • Tier 2 Action: Standard Catalog Inclusion & Organic Search
  • Tier 3 Action: Reposition / Re-shoot in alternative colorway
